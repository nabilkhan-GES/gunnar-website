The fundamentals

What is ranging? Underground GPS — accurate to an inch.

After drilling, a well's exact position is only known approximately — by 10,000 ft, surveys can drift hundreds of feet. That makes intentionally hitting a target wellbore — for a relief well, a blowout intercept or a complex re-entry — practically impossible by conventional means.

01

The problem

All surveying — magnetic or gyro — suffers compounding error. Ellipses of uncertainty routinely reach hundreds of feet and can't be reduced to zero. Intersecting a well by dead reckoning is practically impossible.

02

Our solution

We inject electrical current that travels through rock and energizes the target's steel casing, generating a magnetic signal. By measuring its strength and direction, we know exactly where the target well is — and steer toward it in real time.

03

The payoff

Conventional surveys grow more uncertain with depth. Ranging does the opposite — the error shrinks as you home in on the target, down to within an inch at intercept.
