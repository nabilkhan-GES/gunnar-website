## 1. What the company is

Gunnar Energy Services is an **independent specialist in active magnetic ranging
and wellbore intercept** — relief wells, blowout kills, complex plug & abandonment
(P&A), and energy-transition well placement.

- **Positioning line:** "Built for the wells no one else can save."
- **Hero framing:** "Relief Wells · Magnetic Ranging · Wellbore Intercept" —
  "The independent specialist in active magnetic ranging and wellbore intercept —
  relief wells, blowout kills, complex plug & abandonment, and energy-transition
  placement."
- **Core differentiator — "We range only":** no competing service lines, no vendor
  agenda; the company acts purely in the customer's best interest.
- **Delivery model — one accountable team:** engineering, ranging, directional
  drilling, milling, kill, cement and abandonment under one contract and one
  timeline ("one call, engineering to abandonment").

## 4. Services

Six core service pages, plus two additional named offerings surfaced on the home page.

1. **Relief Wells & Blowout Intercept** — a separate well drilled to
   deterministically intercept and kill an out-of-control well when surface
   intervention has failed.
2. **Plug & Abandonment** — complex and legacy wells (damaged casing, stuck
   packers, lost surface access) sealed via a precise intercepting path, at roughly
   half the rig time.
3. **Well Re-Entry & Milling** — re-entering wells others have written off, using
   magnetic ranging, outside-in milling and directional drilling.
4. **CCUS Well Integrity** — verifiable zonal isolation so every legacy and
   injection well in a carbon-storage complex is sealed and provable.
5. **Geothermal Well Placement** — intentionally connecting wellbores thousands of
   feet underground for closed-loop / next-generation geothermal.
6. **Solution Mining Connections** — precise injection-to-recovery well connections
   for potash, lithium, uranium and salt.

Also presented as named offerings: **Active Magnetic Ranging** (the core
discipline) and **SAGD & CSG Twinning** (collision avoidance / well twinning).

## 5. Proprietary technology

**Hardware:**

- **DeadAhead™** — wired-pipe Active Magnetic Ranging (AMR) While Drilling; range
  up to 500 ft; ~10× the current injection of wireline. World-first commercial
  wired-pipe deployment. U.S. Patent 11,781,421 B2 (reaffirmed by USPTO PTAB, 2025).
- **DeadSet™** — wireline active magnetic ranging; MEMS/solid-state platform;
  engineered for highest probability of first-time intersection.
- **CTRWD™** — coiled-tubing ranging-while-drilling for live-well / under-pressure
  intercepts with full pressure-control integrity.
- **Torped™** — at-bit active magnetic ranging; vendor-neutral, deployable in
  virtually any BHA (twinning, intersection, collision avoidance).
- **EverReady™** — no-access passive magnetic ranging computed from existing
  MWD/wireline steering data; processed remotely.
