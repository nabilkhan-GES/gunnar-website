# Product

## Register
brand

## Users
Engineers and decision-makers on the highest-stakes wells:
- Major oil & gas operators — drilling/well-control engineers facing an active
  blowout, or planning complex/legacy P&A and well re-entry.
- Geothermal developers (incl. closed-loop) needing precise wellbore intersection.
- CCUS and solution-mining operators needing well integrity and intercept.
- Government, regulators and NOCs — orphaned-well P&A and emergency response.
They judge on engineering rigor and proof, not marketing. Context is often urgent
and high-consequence.

## Product Purpose
Gunnar Energy Services is the independent specialist in active magnetic ranging and
wellbore intercept — relief wells, blowout kills, complex P&A, and energy-transition
well placement. The site exists to win trust fast on the highest-stakes wells and
drive two ranked actions: (1) call the 24/7 emergency line for active well control;
(2) request a feasibility review for planned work. Success = qualified operators
making contact.

## Brand Personality
Defense-grade, calm, elite. Voice: precise, expert, unshowy — engineering stated
plainly. Three words: unshakeable, deterministic, independent. The feeling in the
first five seconds is unshakeable competence: the team you call when the well has to
be hit, and they pick up.

## Anti-references
- Generic oilfield-services corporate: stock blue gradients, rig-at-sunset, hard-hat
  clipart, dense corporate-brochure density.
- Cliché SaaS landing: gradient-blob hero, hero-metric template, identical
  feature-card grids.
- Over-designed/flashy: neon, parallax gimmicks, decorative motion.
- Editorial-magazine affectation (display-serif + drop caps) — wrong register here.

## Design Principles
1. Proof over promises — lead with documented, regulator-witnessed results;
   anonymized but specific. ("Proof, not promises.")
2. We range only — independent and unbiased; no competing service lines, act purely
   in the customer's interest. This independence is the moat.
3. One accountable call — engineering → ranging → intercept → kill → cement →
   abandonment as one integrated team, never multi-vendor handoffs.
4. Deterministic, not approximate — communicate certainty and method (inch-level
   intercept) for an audience that judges on rigor.
5. Expert confidence — calm authority; show the hardest wells, let the work speak.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text is soft-white (#EAECEF) on near-black for ≥4.5:1
contrast; electric-yellow CTAs carry near-black text. Reduced-motion alternatives
are implemented (reveals are progressive-enhancement, visible by default). Keyboard
focus uses a 2px yellow focus ring.
